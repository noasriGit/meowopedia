Meowopedia is committed to making our website accessible and usable for as many people as possible. We aim to follow generally recognized accessibility standards, including [WCAG 2.1 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/quickref/) and [WCAG 2.2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/) where practical.

This statement describes our ongoing accessibility work. It is not a guarantee of legal compliance or a certification of perfection.

## Our approach

We design Meowopedia as a reference encyclopedia that should be usable with:

- **Keyboard navigation** — including search, navigation menus, article links, and interactive components such as FAQs.
- **Screen readers and assistive technology** — using semantic HTML, descriptive link text, and appropriate labels where needed.
- **Readable contrast and typography** — with attention to text size, spacing, and color contrast.
- **Reduced motion preferences** — respecting `prefers-reduced-motion` for non-essential animations.
- **Mobile and responsive layouts** — so content remains reachable and readable across screen sizes.

Accessibility is considered alongside our editorial, SEO, and design goals. We improve the site iteratively as we add content and features.

## Measures we take

- Semantic page structure with landmarks such as header, navigation, main content, and footer.
- Descriptive page titles and meta descriptions.
- Accessible search and navigation patterns.
- Structured article pages with headings, table of contents links, and accessible FAQ accordions.
- External links that open in a new tab are indicated to assistive technology where applicable.

## Known limitations

Some areas may still need manual review or further improvement:

- **Third-party content** — Embedded tools, analytics, or future widgets may not fully meet our accessibility targets until reviewed separately.
- **Legacy or imported content** — Long-form articles and tables are updated over time; some pages may need additional alt text, heading review, or table markup improvements.
- **Automated testing gaps** — Automated tools cannot catch every real-world barrier experienced by users.

If you encounter a specific issue, please tell us so we can prioritize a fix.

## Feedback and assistance

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of this website, or if you need content in an alternative format, please contact us:

- **Email:** [hello@meowopedia.com](mailto:hello@meowopedia.com)
- **Contact page:** [About Meowopedia](/about)

We aim to respond within a reasonable timeframe and will work with you to provide the information or assistance you need.

## Formal complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may wish to contact a relevant disability rights or consumer protection organization in your jurisdiction. We welcome constructive feedback and treat accessibility reports seriously.

## Continuous improvement

We periodically review site templates, shared components, and high-traffic pages for accessibility improvements. This statement was last updated on the date shown above and will be revised as our practices evolve.
