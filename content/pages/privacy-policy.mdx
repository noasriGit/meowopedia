We may collect information you voluntarily submit, such as:

When you visit the Site, we and our service providers may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

- IP address (which may be used to derive approximate location)

We use collected information to:

- Operate, maintain, and improve the Site

We do not sell your personal information.

## Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We may use:

- **Essential cookies** — Required for basic Site functionality (e.g., theme preference)
- **Analytics cookies** — To understand how visitors use the Site (e.g., page views, navigation paths)

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some Site features.

We may use third-party services that process data on our behalf, such as:

Our articles may link to external websites (veterinary resources, research papers, etc.). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party sites. Review their policies before submitting personal information.

## Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. Contact correspondence is typically retained until the matter is resolved and for a reasonable period thereafter.

## Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

- Access information we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data

To exercise these rights, contact [hello@meowopedia.com](mailto:hello@meowopedia.com). We may need to verify your identity before responding.

### California residents

If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect and the right to request deletion. We do not sell personal information as defined by the CCPA.

### European Economic Area and UK residents

If you are in the EEA or UK, you may have rights under the GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## Children's privacy

The Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.
