Meowopedia is a feline encyclopedia built for depth, accuracy, and discovery — not a blog, not a listicle site, and not a collection of disconnected articles.

## Our mission

We aim to be the most comprehensive, trustworthy reference about cats on the internet. Every article is written to answer a real question cat owners and enthusiasts search for, then connected to related topics through a structured knowledge graph so you can keep learning in context.

Whether you are researching whether a food is safe, understanding a behavior, comparing breeds, or tracing symptoms to possible conditions, Meowopedia is designed to help you find authoritative answers quickly and explore what matters next.

## Encyclopedia, not blog

Most pet content online is published as one-off posts with no lasting structure. Meowopedia works differently:

- **Entity-based articles** — Each page covers one topic (a breed, food, behavior, disease, symptom, etc.) and is maintained over time.
- **Interconnected knowledge graph** — Articles link to related entities automatically: foods connect to toxic plants, symptoms connect to diseases, behaviors connect to care guides.
- **Search-intent first** — Titles and structure match how people actually search, with dedicated sections for keyword variants rather than vague overviews.
- **Periodic review** — Articles carry `lastReviewed` dates and are updated when veterinary guidance or consensus changes.

## How we ensure quality

All Meowopedia content follows documented [editorial standards](/editorial-standards). In brief:

- We cite real, verifiable sources — veterinary hospitals, universities, poison control centers, and peer-reviewed research.
- Health and medical content is reviewed by credentialed veterinarians before publication.
- We do not invent statistics, studies, or citations.
- We avoid filler writing and answer the question directly.

For important context on how to use health information on this site, read our [medical disclaimer](/medical-disclaimer).

## Who we are

Meowopedia is edited by the **Meowopedia Editorial Team** — feline encyclopedia editors focused on research, structure, and accuracy. Health content receives additional review from licensed veterinarians with relevant specialties.

We are independent publishers. Our goal is to earn your trust through transparent sourcing, clear writing, and consistent updates — not through hype or clickbait.

## Contact and corrections

Found an error or outdated information? We take corrections seriously. Email us at [hello@meowopedia.com](mailto:hello@meowopedia.com) with the article URL and a description of the issue. We review all reports and update articles when warranted.
