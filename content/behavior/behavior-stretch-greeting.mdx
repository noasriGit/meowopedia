Greeting stretches are the feline version of "I see you, I am awake, and I am ready to interact."

## Why this behavior appears at greetings

- Activates muscles after resting
- Marks transition into social interaction
- Lowers tension through predictable ritual

## Distinguish friendly stretch from discomfort

Friendly stretch: fluid movement, forward approach, normal facial expression.
Concern signs: stiffness, asymmetry, flinch, hiding after movement.

## Owner response

Let the cat finish stretching before petting. Reward calm, voluntary approach rather than reaching in early.
