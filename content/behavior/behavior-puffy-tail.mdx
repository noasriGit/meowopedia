A puffed "bottlebrush" tail is caused by piloerection, where small muscles raise hairs to make the body look larger.

## Why the body does this

Piloerection is part of the sympathetic nervous system's fast arousal response. It can serve defensive display, emotional expression, or high-intensity play communication.

## Context guide

| Context | Likely state |
|---|---|
| Backed into corner, hissing | Defensive fear |
| Sideways hops, quick pauses | Excited play |
| Staring at outside trigger | Territorial arousal |

## Best response

Increase distance, reduce trigger exposure, and avoid direct handling until posture normalizes.
