Purring is one of the most misunderstood cat behaviors because it appears in both calm and stressful contexts.

## The short answer

Cats purr to regulate internal state and social communication. Sometimes that state is contentment. Sometimes it is discomfort.

## Read purring in context

| With this body language | More likely meaning |
|---|---|
| Slow blink, loose posture, seeking contact | Social comfort |
| Hiding, reduced appetite, hunched posture | Self-soothing under stress |
| During handling with tail flicks | Rising overstimulation |

## Common purring scenarios

- Greeting a trusted person
- Nursing and maternal care
- Kneading soft surfaces (`behavior-making-biscuits`)
- Veterinary visits or pain episodes

## When to call your vet

If purring changes suddenly and is paired with breathing effort, withdrawal, appetite drop, or altered mobility, treat it as a medical clue rather than a reassurance signal.
