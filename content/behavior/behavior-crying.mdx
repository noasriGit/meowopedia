"Crying" in cats usually means vocal distress sounds, not human-style emotional tears.

## What crying-like sounds can mean

- Requesting food, access, or social contact
- Frustration or conflict in multi-cat homes
- Reproductive calling in intact cats
- Pain or illness

## Fast context check

1. **When** does it happen (night, before meals, near doors)?
2. **How** does it sound (short meow vs prolonged yowl)?
3. **What else** changed (appetite, litter box, activity)?

## Practical response

Address needs on schedule, enrich the environment, and avoid rewarding persistent demand vocalization in unsafe ways. Always investigate sudden behavior shifts medically first.
