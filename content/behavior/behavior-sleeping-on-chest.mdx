When cats choose your chest, they are usually selecting a high-value rest site: warm, elevated, and socially meaningful.

## Four reasons cats pick your chest

1. **Heat:** torso warmth is stable and appealing.
2. **Safety:** sleeping on a trusted human lowers vigilance.
3. **Rhythm:** breathing and heartbeat can be calming.
4. **Bond maintenance:** close contact reinforces social connection.

## Is it always okay?

For most healthy adults, yes. But if you have breathing issues, chest pressure discomfort, or disrupted sleep, set boundaries without punishment.

## Gentle boundary strategy

Create an adjacent "better bed": soft blanket, your scent, mild heat source, and rewards for settling there. Consistency matters more than one-night success.
