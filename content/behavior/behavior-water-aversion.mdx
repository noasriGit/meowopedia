"Cats hate water" is an overgeneralization. A better statement: many cats dislike sudden immersion and loss of control.

## Why water feels unpleasant to many cats

- Wet fur increases heat loss
- Coat weight and cling alter movement
- Slippery surfaces remove traction
- Bath handling can feel inescapable

## How to reduce water stress

Use gradual desensitization, non-slip mats, lukewarm water, and short sessions. Pair calm handling with rewards. Never force a full bath for routine grooming unless needed.

## Longhair context

Longhaired cats, including some Persians, may occasionally need targeted hygiene bathing around soiled areas. Breed grooming profiles are covered in `breed-persian`.

<VetTip title="Not all water behavior is aversion">
Many cats enjoy pawing at faucets or fountains. Curiosity toward moving water does not mean they will accept baths.
</VetTip>
