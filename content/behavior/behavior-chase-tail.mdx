Tail chasing sits on a spectrum: playful practice at one end, medical/behavioral concern at the other.

## Harmless pattern

Short playful bursts, no skin damage, easy redirection to toys, normal behavior between episodes.

## Concerning pattern

- Repetitive episodes daily
- Tail-base licking/biting or hair loss
- Vocalizing or agitation
- Difficult to interrupt

## What to check first

Parasites, skin irritation, anal gland discomfort, pain, and stressors in the home environment.

<HealthAlertBox title="Self-injury risk">
Any tail chasing that causes skin damage requires prompt veterinary treatment to prevent infection and escalating compulsive behavior.
</HealthAlertBox>
