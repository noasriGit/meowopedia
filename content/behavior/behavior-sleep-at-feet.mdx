Sleeping at your feet is a practical social strategy: your cat stays close to you without giving up route control.

## Why feet are popular

- Warmth without chest pressure
- Less hand movement disturbance
- Easy exit path from bed edge
- Stable nightly routine cue

## If foot attacks happen at night

This is usually predatory play targeting movement under blankets. Prevent it with intense evening wand play, then a small meal.

## Comfort setup

Place a soft throw at the bed foot to create a consistent target zone. Reward settling there.
