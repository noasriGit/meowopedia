Distinguishing play from fighting is mostly about **balance and recovery**.

## Play usually includes

- Role switching (both chase and retreat)
- Brief pauses and re-engagement
- Loose, springy body movement
- Minimal vocalization

## Fighting usually includes

- One-sided pursuit and cornering
- Stiff postures and piloerection
- Growling, shrieking, repeated hissing
- No recovery pauses

## Decision table

| If you see | Treat as |
|---|---|
| Mutual pause and reset | Play |
| One cat hiding and refusing to re-emerge | Conflict |
| Fur flying or puncture wounds | Fight |

## Safe interruption

Use a barrier, blanket drop nearby, or noise away from cats. Do not reach into a fight with your hands.
