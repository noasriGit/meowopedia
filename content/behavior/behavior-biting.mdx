Cat bites are communication with teeth. The key is to map **which type** of bite is happening.

## Bite types at a glance

| Pattern | Typical trigger | Intensity |
|---|---|---|
| Petting bite | Overstimulation | Mild to moderate |
| Play bite | Predatory play | Usually inhibited |
| Fear/defensive bite | Threat perception | Moderate to severe |
| Redirected bite | Trigger elsewhere (window cat, noise) | Can be severe |

## "Bite when petting" pattern

Watch for early signs: skin ripple, tail lash, ears rotating sideways, head turn toward your hand. Stop before the bite, then reward calm recovery.

## "Love bites"

Some cats give brief inhibited nips during grooming-like interaction. If pressure increases or punctures skin, treat it as an overstimulation issue, not affection.

## Prevention plan

- Keep petting sessions short and predictable.
- Offer wand play to drain predatory energy.
- Avoid hand-play that teaches skin as toy.
- Build choice: let the cat initiate and leave.

<HealthAlertBox title="Any bite that breaks skin">
Wash immediately and seek human medical advice. Cat bites can cause deep tissue infection even when the puncture looks small.
</HealthAlertBox>
