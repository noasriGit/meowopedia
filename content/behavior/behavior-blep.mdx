A blep is a tiny tongue-out pause that looks funny but is usually normal.

## Common benign blep moments

- After intense grooming
- During drowsy transitions
- After flehmen-like scent processing
- During relaxed social contact

## When bleps are not "just cute"

Persistent tongue protrusion may point to oral pain, trauma, neurologic change, or jaw issues.

<VetTip title="Duration is the key clue">
A few seconds is usually benign. Minutes to hours, especially with drooling or poor appetite, needs veterinary evaluation.
</VetTip>
