Night meowing is one of the most common owner complaints because it blends normal feline biology with learned household patterns.

## Major causes

### Biological activity peaks
Cats are naturally more active around dawn and dusk.

### Learned reinforcement
If meowing reliably gets food, play, or door access, the behavior strengthens.

### Stress and social change
New pets, moves, and resource competition can increase nighttime vocalization.

### Medical causes
Pain, cognitive dysfunction, endocrine disease, and sensory decline are common in seniors.

## 7-day reset plan

- Increase evening play intensity.
- Feed a substantial final meal before bedtime.
- Use automatic overnight feeder if hunger-driven.
- Ignore attention-seeking calls when safe.
- Reward quiet behavior at neutral times.

<HealthAlertBox title="Senior cats need diagnostics">
New nighttime yowling in older cats warrants a veterinary exam and lab screening rather than behavior-only treatment.
</HealthAlertBox>
