It is common for owners to check a cat's ears first when they suspect fever. Ear touch can give a clue, but it is not accurate enough to diagnose illness on its own.

## Why Ears Feel Hot or Cold

Cats regulate heat partly through blood flow in thin ear tissue. Ear temperature can shift with:

- room temperature
- sunlight exposure
- recent activity or stress
- resting blood flow changes

So both warm ears and mildly cool ears can be normal at times.

## Normal vs Concerning Patterns

| Pattern | Likely meaning |
|------|---|
| Brief warmth after play | Often normal |
| Warm ears plus low energy/appetite | Possible fever or illness |
| Warm painful ears with scratching | Possible ear infection/inflammation |
| Very cold ears plus weakness | Possible poor perfusion or low body temperature |

## Normal vs Emergency

<HealthAlertBox title="Emergency signs with ear temperature changes">
Seek urgent care if ear temperature changes occur with collapse, severe lethargy, pale or blue gums, labored breathing, seizures, or toxin exposure concerns.
</HealthAlertBox>

For non-emergency cases, call your vet if warm or cold ears persist with behavior or appetite changes.

## Best Next Step

Take a true body temperature using vet-approved technique, and track other symptoms (appetite, energy, litter box changes). Ear feel alone should not guide treatment decisions.
