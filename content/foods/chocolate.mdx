## Can Cats Eat It?

**No.** Chocolate is toxic to cats and should never be offered intentionally.

<HealthAlertBox title="Emergency">
If your cat has ingested chocolate, contact your veterinarian or ASPCA Animal Poison Control immediately. Do not wait for symptoms.
</HealthAlertBox>

## Why Chocolate Is Toxic

Chocolate contains **theobromine** and **caffeine**, methylxanthines that cats metabolize slowly. These compounds stimulate the cardiovascular and nervous systems, potentially causing:

- Vomiting and diarrhea
- Hyperactivity and restlessness
- Tachycardia (rapid heart rate)
- Tremors and seizures
- Death in severe cases

## Toxicity by Chocolate Type

| Type | Theobromine Level | Risk |
|------|-------------------|------|
| White chocolate | Low | Still avoid — fat/sugar risk |
| Milk chocolate | Moderate | High |
| Dark chocolate | High | Very high |
| Baking chocolate | Very high | Potentially fatal |

## What To Do If Ingested

1. Note the type and estimated amount consumed
2. Call your veterinarian or poison control
3. Do not induce vomiting unless directed by a professional
4. Monitor for symptoms and follow veterinary guidance

## Safe Alternatives

Offer cat-specific treats formulated for feline nutrition. Small amounts of plain cooked meat are safer occasional options — see our safe treats guide for vetted alternatives.

<VetTip title="Hidden Sources">
Chocolate appears in baked goods, ice cream, protein bars, and cocoa-flavored products. Keep all cocoa-containing items secured.
</VetTip>
